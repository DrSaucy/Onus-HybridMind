## 🤖 SLIDE 4: The Architecture (1:45 - 2:30)
**Visual:** Slide 4 ("Three Agents. One Verdict.")  
**Presenter Tone:** Technical, authoritative, proving architectural depth.

**Spoken Script:**
> "To achieve absolute accuracy without hallucination, we designed an event-driven, three-agent architecture orchestrated via LlamaIndex Workflows.
>
> First, our **Executor Agent** receives a natural language query, writes raw SQL against our PostgreSQL database, and simultaneously queries our ChromaDB vector store to extract verbatim contract clauses.
>
> Second, our **Verifier Agent** takes those raw numbers and legal clauses, applies strict mathematical logic, and produces a structured JSON verdict.
>
> Finally, our **Chronicler Agent** packages the verified finding into a cryptographically signed audit event and broadcasts the exact dollar amount owed. Each agent passes structured context forward—never raw prompts—guaranteeing a fully auditable chain of reasoning."
